# Supplier Invoice Processing

## Receipt
Invoices arrive in the accounts payable mailbox and are matched to purchase orders.

## Matching
A three-way match compares the order, the goods received note, and the invoice before payment is released.

## Payment runs
Payments run weekly on Thursdays. Bank detail changes require a call-back to a known contact before they are applied.
