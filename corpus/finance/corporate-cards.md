# Corporate Card Policy

## Eligibility
Cards are issued to employees who regularly incur business costs, with manager approval.

## Use
Use the card only for company expenses. Personal use is a policy breach and must be repaid immediately.

## Reconciliation
Match every transaction to a receipt in the expense tool by the monthly deadline. Report a lost card to the bank and Finance at once.
