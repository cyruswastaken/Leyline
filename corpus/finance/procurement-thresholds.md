# Procurement Approval Thresholds

## Approval levels
Purchases under one thousand need manager approval. Up to twenty thousand needs a department head. Above that requires the finance director and a competitive quote.

## Preferred suppliers
Use approved suppliers where available. New suppliers complete a due diligence questionnaire.

## Splitting orders
Splitting a purchase to avoid a threshold is prohibited.
