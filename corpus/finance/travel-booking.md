# Booking Business Travel

## Use the travel portal
Book flights and hotels through the travel portal so rates and duty-of-care tracking apply.

## Class of travel
Economy class applies for flights under six hours. Longer flights may use premium economy with director approval.

## Changes
Cancel unused bookings promptly. Unused tickets are credited to the company account.
