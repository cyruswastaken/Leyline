# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## What is covered
Reasonable travel, accommodation, and client meals incurred for company business.

## Submitting claims
Submit receipts in the expense tool within thirty days. Claims without receipts are rejected.

## Limits
Meals are capped per person per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed. Your manager approves claims before payment.
