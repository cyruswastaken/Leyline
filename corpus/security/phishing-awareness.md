# Spotting and Reporting Phishing

## Warning signs
Unexpected urgency, mismatched sender addresses, requests for credentials, and unusual attachments.

## What to do
Do not click. Use the report button in the mail client so Security can remove the message from other mailboxes.

## If you clicked
Tell the Helpdesk straight away and change your password. Quick reporting limits the damage.
