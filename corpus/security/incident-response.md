# Security Incident Response Playbook

## Detect and triage
Classify the alert by severity. A confirmed compromise of credentials or data is severity one and pages the on-call lead.

## Contain
Isolate affected hosts, disable compromised accounts, and preserve logs before any rebuild.

## Eradicate and recover
Remove the root cause, restore from clean backups, and monitor closely for reinfection.

## Review
Hold a blameless review within five working days and track the fixes to completion.
