# Privileged Access Management

## Principles
Administrators use separate accounts for administration and are granted rights only for the time needed.

## Approval and review
Elevated access requires a ticket and a second approver. All privileged sessions are recorded and reviewed monthly.

## Break-glass accounts
Emergency accounts are sealed and any use triggers an immediate review.
