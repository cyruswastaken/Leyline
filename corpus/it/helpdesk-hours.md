# IT Helpdesk Hours and Priorities

## Hours
The Helpdesk is staffed from 7:00 to 19:00 on working days. Urgent security incidents are covered around the clock through the on-call phone.

## Priorities
Outages affecting many people come first, followed by blocked individuals, then general requests.

## Raising a ticket
Describe what you were doing, the exact error text, and when it started. Screenshots speed up diagnosis.
