# Setting Up and Using Printers

## Adding a printer
Open Settings, choose Printers, and add the printer named after your floor. Printing from a laptop requires a network connection or the VPN.

## Secure release
Print jobs wait in a queue until you tap your badge on the printer. Jobs are deleted after twenty-four hours.

## Paper jams
Open the side tray, gently remove the jammed sheet, and close the tray firmly. If the jam repeats, report the printer asset tag to the Helpdesk.
