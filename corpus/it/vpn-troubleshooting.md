# Troubleshooting VPN Connection Problems

## Cannot connect
Check that your internet connection works, then restart the VPN client. Confirm the gateway name matches the one shown in the setup guide.

## Connected but slow
Close video calls you are not using and move closer to the router. Large downloads should use the shared drive instead.

## Authentication errors
Make sure the clock on your laptop is correct, since a wrong time breaks one-time codes. If the error continues, contact the Helpdesk with the exact message.
