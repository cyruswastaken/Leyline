# Enrolling in Multi-Factor Authentication

## Why it is required
Every employee account must use multi-factor authentication. A stolen password alone cannot open your account.

## Enrollment steps
1. Install the approved authenticator app on your phone.
2. Sign in to the security portal and choose "Add a method".
3. Scan the QR code with the app and enter the six-digit code to confirm.
4. Save the recovery codes in the company password manager.

## Replacing a phone
Enroll the new phone before wiping the old one. If the old phone is lost, ask the Helpdesk to reset your enrollment.
