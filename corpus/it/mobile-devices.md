# Using Your Phone for Work

## Enrollment
Install the company portal app to receive work email and calendar. Enrollment adds a separate protected work profile.

## Requirements
A screen lock and device encryption are required. The company can remove only work data from a personal phone.

## Lost phone
Report a lost or stolen phone to the Helpdesk immediately so work data can be erased remotely.
