# Resetting Your Corporate Password

## Self-service reset
Open the sign-in page and choose "Forgot password". Enter your work email and approve the prompt sent to your enrolled authenticator app. Choose a new password that is at least fourteen characters long.

## If self-service fails
Contact the IT Helpdesk and verify your identity with your manager's name and employee number. The Helpdesk never asks for your current password and will never send a reset link by text message.

## After the reset
Sign out of every device, then sign in again with the new password. Update saved passwords on your phone and mail client to avoid account lockouts.
