# Getting Access to Shared Drives

## Requesting access
Ask the folder owner to approve access. IT grants it once the owner confirms in the service portal.

## Rules
Keep confidential files in the team folder, not in personal storage. Do not share links outside the company without approval.

## Recovering deleted files
Deleted files stay in the recycle bin for thirty days. After that, ask the Helpdesk to restore from backup.
