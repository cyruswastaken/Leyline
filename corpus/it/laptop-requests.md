# Requesting or Replacing a Laptop

## New starters
Laptops are ordered by IT after HR confirms the start date. Standard models ship within five working days.

## Replacements
Laptops are refreshed every four years. Request an early replacement through the service portal if the device is damaged or too slow to work on.

## Returning old hardware
Back up your files to the shared drive, then hand the laptop to IT. Do not wipe it yourself; IT erases drives to a certified standard.
