# Email and Calendar Basics

## Mailbox size
Each mailbox holds fifty gigabytes. Archive old messages to keep the mailbox responsive.

## Shared calendars
Team calendars are managed by team owners. Ask the owner to grant you edit or view access.

## Suspicious messages
Report phishing using the report button in the mail client. Do not click links or open attachments from unknown senders.
