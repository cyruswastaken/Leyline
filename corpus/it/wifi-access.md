# Connecting to Office Wi-Fi

## Employee network
Choose the Corp-Secure network and sign in with your corporate email and password. Your laptop receives a certificate automatically the first time.

## Guest network
Visitors use the Guest network and a daily voucher issued at reception. Guest devices cannot reach internal systems.

## Troubleshooting
If the connection drops, forget the network, restart the Wi-Fi adapter and reconnect. Report persistent problems with the floor and desk number so the network team can check the access point.
