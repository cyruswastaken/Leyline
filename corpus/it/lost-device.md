# Reporting a Lost or Stolen Device

## Act immediately
Call the Helpdesk as soon as you notice the loss, day or night. Tell them what the device was, where you last had it, and whether it was locked.

## What IT does
IT disables the account sessions, locks the device remotely, and starts a data loss assessment with Security.

## Afterwards
File a police report for theft. IT issues a replacement once the report number is recorded.
