# Video Conferencing Guide

## Starting a meeting
Create a meeting from the calendar so attendees receive the invitation and link automatically.

## Audio and video problems
Select the correct microphone and speaker in the meeting settings. Headsets give the best audio quality.

## Recording
Ask attendees for consent before recording. Recordings are stored for ninety days and then deleted.
