# Emergency Evacuation Procedure

## When the alarm sounds
Stop work, leave belongings, and follow the green exit signs. Do not use lifts.

## Assembly point
Gather at the marked assembly point and wait for the fire warden to take a headcount.

## Returning
Do not re-enter until the warden confirms the building is safe.
