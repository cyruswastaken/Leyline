# Hot Desk Booking

## Booking
Reserve a desk the day before using the workplace app. Bookings can be changed until 8:00 that morning.

## Clean desk
Leave the desk clear and take belongings with you at the end of the day.

## Accessibility
Adjustable desks and quiet zones can be reserved by contacting Facilities.
