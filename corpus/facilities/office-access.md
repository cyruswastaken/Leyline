# Office Access and Badges

## Getting a badge
New starters collect a badge from reception on the first day. Photo ID is required.

## Using the badge
Tap the badge at every door. Do not lend your badge or hold doors open for people you do not recognize.

## Lost badge
Report a lost badge to reception at once so it can be deactivated.
