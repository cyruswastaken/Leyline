# Parking and Bicycle Storage

## Parking permits
Permits are limited and allocated by lottery each quarter. Carpooling employees receive priority.

## Bicycles
Secure bicycle storage and showers are on the ground floor. Register your bicycle with reception.

## Electric vehicles
Charging bays are available on a first-come basis. Move your vehicle once it is charged.
