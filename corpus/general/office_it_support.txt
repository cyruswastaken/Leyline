Document Title: IT Helpdesk Support Guide

Document ID: GEN-IT-001

Access Level: general

The IT Helpdesk assists employees with common technical issues.

Supported requests include:

- VPN connection problems
- Corporate account access
- Password-reset assistance
- Approved software installation
- Workstation configuration issues

When contacting the IT Helpdesk, employees should provide a clear
description of the problem.

Employees should never send passwords, authentication codes, or
other sensitive credentials in support requests.
