Document Title: Corporate Password Policy

Document ID: GEN-PASSWORD-001

Access Level: general

Corporate accounts must use strong passwords.

Passwords should:

- Be unique to the corporate account.
- Not be reused across personal services.
- Not be shared with other employees.
- Not contain easily guessed personal information.

Employees should use the approved corporate password-management
solution when storing credentials.

If an employee believes their password has been compromised, they
should immediately change the password and contact the IT Helpdesk.

IT personnel will never ask employees to provide their password
through email or chat.
