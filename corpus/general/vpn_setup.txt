Document Title: Corporate VPN Setup Guide

Document ID: GEN-VPN-001

Access Level: general

The corporate VPN allows employees to securely access approved
internal services while working outside the corporate network.

To connect to the corporate VPN:

1. Open the approved corporate VPN client.
2. Enter the corporate VPN gateway provided by IT.
3. Sign in using your corporate credentials.
4. Complete multi-factor authentication if prompted.
5. Verify that the VPN client reports a connected status.

If the VPN connection fails, contact the IT Helpdesk.

Employees should not share their VPN credentials or multi-factor
authentication codes with anyone.
