# Salary Bands and Pay Review

## Bands
Each role family has a band with a minimum, midpoint and maximum. New hires normally start between the minimum and midpoint.

## Annual review
Pay reviews follow the performance cycle. Budgets are set by Finance and distributed by department.

## Handling data
Individual salaries are restricted to HR and the approving manager. Never email salary spreadsheets.
