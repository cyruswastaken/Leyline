# Disciplinary Procedure

## Stages
Informal discussion, written warning, final written warning, then dismissal. Serious misconduct may skip stages.

## Fair process
The employee receives written notice of the allegation, may bring a colleague to the hearing, and may appeal.

## Records
Case files are confidential and held by HR for the retention period set by law.
