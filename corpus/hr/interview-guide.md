# Interviewing Guide for Hiring Managers

## Structure
Use the same core questions for every candidate and score answers against the agreed criteria.

## Fairness
Do not ask about age, family plans, health, or religion. Record notes only about job-related evidence.

## Decisions
Share scores with HR within two working days. Offers are issued by HR, not by interviewers.
