# Company Calendar and All-Hands Schedule

## All-hands meetings
Held on the first Thursday of each month at 10:00 and recorded for those who cannot attend.

## Company holidays
The office closes between Christmas and New Year. Dates for each year are published in January.

## Team offsites
Departments schedule offsites with their directors and book venues through Facilities.
