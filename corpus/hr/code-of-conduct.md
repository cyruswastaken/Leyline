# Code of Conduct

## Respect
Treat colleagues, customers and partners with courtesy. Harassment and discrimination are never acceptable.

## Conflicts of interest
Declare outside work or financial interests that could affect your judgment.

## Speaking up
Raise concerns with your manager, HR, or the anonymous ethics line. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.
