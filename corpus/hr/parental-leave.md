# Parental Leave

## Eligibility
All employees may take parental leave after the birth or adoption of a child, regardless of length of service.

## Duration
Twenty weeks of leave at full pay are available to each parent, taken within the first twelve months.

## Returning
Agree a return plan with your manager and HR at least one month before you come back. Phased returns are available.
