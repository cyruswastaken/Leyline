# Performance Review Process

## Cycle
Reviews run twice a year. Managers collect peer feedback, draft ratings, and calibrate with HR before sharing results.

## Ratings
Ratings use a five-point scale. Calibration sessions compare ratings across teams to keep them consistent.

## Confidentiality
Draft ratings and calibration notes are confidential to HR and the manager chain until the final conversation.
