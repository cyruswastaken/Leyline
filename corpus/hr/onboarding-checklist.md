# New Starter Onboarding Checklist

## Before day one
HR confirms the contract, IT prepares the laptop and accounts, and Facilities arranges the desk and badge.

## First week
Complete security awareness training, meet your team, and agree goals for the first ninety days with your manager.

## First month
Finish compliance modules, enroll in benefits, and schedule a check-in with your buddy.
