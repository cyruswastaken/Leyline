# Annual Leave Policy

## Entitlement
Full-time employees receive twenty-five days of paid annual leave per year, plus public holidays.

## Booking leave
Request leave in the HR portal at least two weeks ahead for more than three days. Your manager approves or declines within three working days.

## Carry over
Up to five unused days carry into the next year and must be used by the end of March.
